Where this comes from

Every place here is licensed by one of the UK kashrut authorities listed below — nothing is included on reputation, and kosher-style or unsupervised vegetarian places are deliberately left out. Supervision changes often, and a licence can lapse between visits. Tap the badge on any listing to open that authority's own directory and confirm before you order.

Supermarket entries are a different thing and are labelled as such: the products carry their own hechsher, the store is not supervised. Check each packet. Most Waitrose branches also stock KLBD-certified challah and sliced rye from Buckingham Boulangerie, and a number of Hovis and Kingsmill loaves carry KLBD or Federation certification wherever you buy them.

  • KF Kosher — Kehillas FederationLicensed establishments & catering. Supervises Reuben's on Baker Street.
